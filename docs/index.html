What costs the most

01

Rewrite the Experience bullets

+14

Nine of eleven bullets are duties. Turning even six into duty→outcome lines with numbers you already have is the single biggest gain — and the facts are already on the page, so this is the fastest points too.

02

Rewrite the About

+9

The current three sentences describe a role. A specific, first-person About with one proof point per paragraph moves Profile from Good toward Strong.

03

Start a light posting cadence

+6

One post a fortnight on the niche lifts Content off the floor. It ranks below the rewrites because the points arrive slowly — you have to publish for weeks before they land.
